Frequently Asked Trade Questions

Everything you need to know about bulk ordering at City Plaza Complex, Hyderabad

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) per design set?

Our standard MOQ is 1 complete set/bundle per design (typically 4 to 6 pieces across size ratios like 18x22 or 24x34). For festive cash discount schemes, orders start at ₹50,000.

How do size ratios work for Bombay Frocks & Sharara suits?

Each design is packed in pre-assorted size and color bundles (e.g. sizes 22 to 34). Colors in each bundle are mixed across available design shades.

Which transport carriers do you use for AP & Telangana delivery?

We dispatch daily from Dewan Devdi via leading transport partners including Navata Road Transport, Kranti Express, and V-Trans, serving towns across AP, Telangana, Karnataka, and Maharashtra.

How can out-station retailers view locked wholesale pricing?

To protect retail profit margins for our shopkeeper clients, public visitors only see product lookbooks. Wholesale quotations and rates are shared directly after submitting your selected codes and shop details via WhatsApp.
